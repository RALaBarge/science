- **Drag back in time**: replaced the computed (rotated modern outlines) map with real PALEOMAP reconstructions (Scotese et al.), Mollweide maps at 5, 60, 105, 155, 190, 225, 250 Ma, 7 steps (slider + chips + play). - **Ring of Fire**: data was already real (NOAA NCEI volcanoes, USGS M7+ quakes, Muller 2019 boundaries), so I kept it. Removed the faint internal land lines on that map.
- **Rock cycle crayons**: kept the SVGs and added a real photo under each: basalt (igneous), Zion sandstone layers (sedimentary), garnet gneiss (metamorphic).
- **Cool slowly = big crystals**: the three slider images are now real photos (obsidian, basalt, granite). "Rocks you can find" now has a 6-photo gallery (granite, basalt, sandstone, Petoskey stone, marble, slate) replacing the texture drawings.
- **Oxbow / river**: removed the AI aerial "real river loop" photo. Added "Real rivers that moved": Yellow River course-changes map (CC BY-SA 4.0), Fisk 1944 Mississippi meander-belt map (public domain), Landsat 7 Mississippi (public domain, false color, stated in caption). The simulation is labeled a simple model. No usable Yangtze historic-course image found on Commons, so Yellow River + Mississippi are used instead.
- **Michigan shaped by ice**: replaced the two AI photos with real ones: glacial erratic (Bunker Nature Preserve, Michigan), glacial grooves (Kelleys Island, Ohio, Lake Erie; captioned as same ice sheet), plus a new "What the ice left behind" row: Great Lakes from space (NASA SeaWiFS), Sleeping Bear Dunes, kettle lake (Glacial Lakes State Park, Minnesota; captioned as such, no good Michigan one found). Removed the "computer-generated" notes; footer updated to say which images are still AI (pudding etc.).
