How to play and the science

Click a tile to turn it a quarter turn clockwise (right-click or Shift-click turns it back). Connect the battery’s + side, through every bulb, back to its − side before the clock runs out. Click switches to turn them ON. Keys: arrow keys move a cursor, Space turns the tile.

Electricity only flows around a complete loop, called a circuit. Copper conducts; plastic is an insulator, so a plastic piece is a dead end. An OFF switch is a gap.

This game really works out the electricity. Bulbs on one loop are in series: they share the battery’s push, so two bulbs each glow at half brightness, and three is the most that still light. If you join + to − with plain wire, the current takes that easy path and skips the bulbs: a short circuit. It costs 5 seconds here, and in real wiring it makes wires dangerously hot.

Stars come from how few clicks you used compared with par, the fewest clicks needed. Out of time? Answer 4 questions for 10 seconds each (once per level). Stuck? Answer one question and the game fixes one tile, for one star.
