How to play and the science

You are a blob of water. Move with ←→ (or AD), jump with ↑ or Space, restart with R. Your temperature decides your state, just like real water: at 0°C or colder you are ice, and at 100°C or hotter you are steam.

Orange pads heat you and blue pads cool you. The wavy red air above a heater is hot air that keeps heating you as you rise. The snowy blue air is cold. With nothing around, steam slowly cools until it condenses back to water, and ice slowly warms until it melts.

Each state has powers. Water walks, jumps, and drips through grates, but falls into pools. Ice is slippery and heavy, floats on pools (ice is less dense than water), and smashes cracked blocks if it hits them fast. Steam floats up through grates and gets pushed by vents.

Heat and cold packs (H and C) change your temperature by 110°C in one go. Grab them in levels, or answer 4 questions for more when you run out (once per level). Beat par time with no restarts for 3 stars. The Daily button picks a level of the day, sometimes mirrored.
