How to play and the science

The laser shoots a beam in a straight line. Click an empty square to put a mirror there, click again to turn it the other way, and a third time to take it back. Steer the beam into every target. Keys: arrow keys move a cursor, Space places or turns a mirror.

A mirror turns the beam 90°. Light leaves a mirror at the same angle it came in, so a mirror tilted like / sends a beam heading right up the board, and one tilted like \ sends it down.

Materials behave like real ones. Wood is opaque: it blocks light. Clear glass is transparent: light goes straight through. Frosted glass is translucent: light gets through, but at half strength, and a target needs at least half strength to light up. Color filters only let their own color through, so white light comes out red from a red filter, and red light can't get through a blue one. Beam splitters are half mirrors: half the light bounces off and half goes straight through. Some targets are colored and need light of that color.

Each puzzle has a par: the fewest mirrors the game's solver needed. Match par for 3 stars. You have one spare mirror. Out of mirrors? Answer 4 questions for up to 4 more (once per level). Stuck? Answer one question to see where one mirror goes, but it costs a star.
