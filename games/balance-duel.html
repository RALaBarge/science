How to play and the science

Take turns putting one of your weights on the beam: pick a weight, then click a spot (or use number keys for the weight, ←→ to choose a spot and Space to place). Each spot holds one weight, so the spots near the pivot fill up fast. If your weight makes the beam tip, you lose the round. If every weight goes on and the beam stays up, it’s a draw.

Each weight turns the beam with a force equal to its mass × its distance from the pivot. A 2kg weight 3 spots out turns the beam just as hard as a 3kg weight 2 spots out (both make 6). The gauge at the top adds up the left and right turning forces. When the difference passes the tip limit, over it goes.

Trying to leave the other player with no safe spot is the whole game. Pivot trick: answer one question to slide the pivot one step, once per round. That changes every weight’s distance at once, just like moving a real seesaw’s stand, so it can save you or tip the beam. Rescue: if you have no safe move, answer 4 questions and each right answer lifts the weight doing the most damage off the beam.

Play against a friend or the CPU (the menu by Player 2’s name). Both players always get the same weights, and who goes first switches every round.
