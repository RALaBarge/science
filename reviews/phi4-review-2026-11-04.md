# Phi-4 / Phi-4 Reasoning pass over the lessons (2026-11-04)

Models: `mlx-community/phi-4-4bit` and `mlx-community/Phi-4-reasoning-4bit` on the M1 (oMLX folder `~/mlx/mlx-community/`). Sampling for the reasoning model: temperature 0.8, top_p 0.95, top_k 50 (set in `~/.omlx/model_settings.json`).

Scope: 57 lesson pages (K-5 x24, 6th x14, 7th x9, 8th x9, 9th x1) and the 295 quiz questions on them.

## Quiz answers: 295 of 295 marked answers hold up

Each question was shown to Phi-4 with the options only (the marked answer hidden). Phi-4 matched the marked answer on 293. The two misses were the model's own errors:

| Page | Question | Phi-4 | Phi-4 Reasoning | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| k-5 unit-00 | "Which hypothesis can be tested with a fair test?" | B | C (matches marked) | Marked C is right: B changes three things at once. |
| 8th unit-8.4 | "Lightning, 3 seconds before thunder, is about..." | B (3 km) | B (3 km) | Marked A (1 km) is right: 3 s x 343 m/s is about 1 km. Both models worked out ~1 km and then wrote 3 km. |

This checks that one option is defensible as correct. It does not check distractor quality.

## Page-level review: no real findings

Phi-4 (non-reasoning) read each page and listed FACT / NUMBERS / QUIZ / ACTIVITY / LEVEL problems. Every high-confidence flag I checked against the page text was wrong or trivial:

- 6th unit-06: "energy released 1x vs about 500x" for magnitude 7.0 vs 8.8. Correct: 10^(1.5 x 1.8) is about 500.
- 7th unit-7.3: quoted "An exothermic reaction absorbs energy". The page says the opposite (it is correct); the model misquoted it.
- 7th unit-7.4: "starch to sugar" flagged because the intermediate is maltose. Fine at grade 7.
- 8th unit-8.2: "doubling speed needs four times the height" is correct (v squared = 2gh).
- Many "unsafe" flags (lamps, hot water, rulers, laser pointers) with nothing specific.
- Many "quiz lacks options" flags: the model saw UI text such as "0 of 6 correct" and not the quiz data on pages where the quiz is built from `finalQuiz([...])`.
- 9th unit-9.1: says "a few advertise more than a million" tokens of context; the model calls that outdated. This is the one date-sensitive claim. It may be true that it is stale by now; check a current source.

Per-page outputs were not kept in the repo because they are mostly noise.

## Phi-4 Reasoning as a page reviewer: not usable as run

On a whole page it thinks past 9,000 tokens without finishing (about 11 minutes a page) and, when the thinking is cut at 3,000 tokens, answers "none" in every category. It worked on single questions (about 100 s each when asked to think).

## Practical use

- Fast checker for quiz keys: Phi-4 at about 3 s per question. 295 questions took about 15 minutes. Rerun after any quiz edit: `~/phi_review/quiz_run.py fast` on the M1 (script and `quiz.json` there).
- Not a substitute for a human or a stronger model for fact review of whole pages.
