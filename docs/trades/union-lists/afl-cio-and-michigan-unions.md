## Michigan AFL-CIO affiliates

Page https://miaflcio.org/our-organization/ says it lists the international and statewide unions affiliated with the Michigan State AFL-CIO, but the list is rendered as logo images with no text names in the fetched HTML. Logo filenames include Teamsters and Boilermakers. A text roster was not found. Counts seen in WebSearch snippets (40, 55, 59 unions) come from third-party sites and were not checked against a primary page.
