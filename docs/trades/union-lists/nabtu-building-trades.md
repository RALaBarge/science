# NABTU affiliates: trades and apprenticeship

Source of the affiliate list: https://nabtu.org/about-nabtu/affiliates/ (fetched 2026-11-04 via browserbox fetch.get).
Trade lists and apprenticeship lengths come from each union's own page as cited. "not stated" means the page(s) read did not give a length.

The NABTU affiliates page lists 14 US unions plus the Canadian Building Trades Unions (CBTU, excluded from the US list). Teamsters is listed there as "many in the construction industry" and is included with its own construction division page.

- Teamsters: construction trade list not found; only the division name seen (teamster.org).
- LIUNA: laborer trade/specialty list not found on liuna.org pages read.
- Carpenters (United Brotherhood of Carpenters): not on the NABTU affiliates page as fetched, so not listed. Not verified whether it is an affiliate.
- Painters/IUPAT wallpaper hanging, floor covering, and glazing appear in the NABTU description and in iupat.org trade pages respectively; no separate length found for each.
- BAC homepage and apprentice contest page (bacweb.org) give no apprenticeship length.
- IBEW apprenticeship page (ibew.org/apprenticeship) fetched; it contained no length.
- Ironworkers homepage and apprenticeship URL: no trade list or length found on ironworkers.org pages read.
- Insulators homepage (insulators.org): no trade list or length found; the NABTU page is the only source for insulator trades and hours.
