**What this means for what to build first**: Build first around large, stable, clear-path trades where a student can actually start locally: electricians, HVAC, industrial machinery mechanics, plumbing, welding, construction labor/carpentry, auto service, machining, diesel/mechanical repair, and WCC-linked health technicians.
