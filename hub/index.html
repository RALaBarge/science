Science Arcade play · learn · repeat

Six science games for grades 3–6. Every game runs in a Chromebook browser with no sign-in, plays a demo until you click, and lets you answer science questions to earn bonuses.

Gravity Duel

Two-player artillery around planets. Bend your shot with gravity, knock planets around, try Moon, Magnet and Orbit Golf modes.

Turn wire tiles to connect the battery through every bulb before time runs out. Real circuit math: series bulbs dim, shorts skip them.

Balance Duel

Take turns placing weights on a seesaw. Turning force is weight × distance. Whoever tips it loses. Play a friend or the CPU.
